HOW THEY CONTROL YOUR DRUGS

Formulary Manipulation

PBMs create "preferred drug lists" that favor expensive medications with high rebates over cheaper alternatives. They literally get paid more when your drugs cost more.

Pharmacy Steering

They force you to use their own mail-order pharmacies or affiliated chains, killing independent pharmacies. CVS owns CVS Caremark. UnitedHealth owns OptumRx AND thousands of clinics.

Spread Pricing

They charge your insurance plan $100 for a drug, pay the pharmacy $60, and pocket the $40 difference. Pure profit for doing literally nothing but adding costs.
